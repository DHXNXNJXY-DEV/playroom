Photosensitivity warning

Some games and video wallpapers may contain flashing lights or patterns that can trigger seizures in people with photosensitivity, even if they have never had a seizure before.

Stop playing and consult a doctor if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, disorientation, or convulsions. Take regular breaks, and remove any wallpaper that makes you uncomfortable.
